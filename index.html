        Find your MAC address

        1
        Download nRF Connect

        Free app by Nordic Semiconductor — available on iOS and Android

        2
        Scan for devices

        Open nRF Connect and tap "Scan". Hold your Bluetooth tag close to your phone.

        3
        Copy the address

        Your tag appears as a device. The MAC address looks like AA:BB:CC:DD:EE:FF — copy it and paste it here.
